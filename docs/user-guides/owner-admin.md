# User Guide — Owner / Admin

For: proprietors / partners of Linga Bhairavi Enterprise (system role **admin**). You can see and do everything. Your most important job in this system is not data entry — it is **making sure your team enters their data**, because reports only show what was entered.

> The last quotation CRM failed because staff left entries out ("If it's not entered, how will it come?" — kickoff meeting [26:40–27:05]). This system shows you, person by person, what is pending. Please look at it every week.

## 1. Your daily routine (10 minutes)

1. **Log in** (phone or laptop). A pop-up lists **customer PO lines not yet delivered**. Note any that are overdue and ask store/sales.
2. **Dashboard** — read the tiles from left to right:
   - *Follow-ups due today* — quotations your sales team must call on.
   - *Undelivered lines* — PO items still pending at customer sites.
   - *Ready to invoice* — fully delivered orders not yet billed.
   - *Receivables overdue* / *Payables due this week*.
   - *Refills due (next 10 days)* — extinguisher customers to approach.
   - *Projects* — contract value vs spend, amber at 80 % of budget, red at 100 %.
   - *Pending entries by person* — the accountability tile.
3. **Approvals** (bell icon): quotations below minimum margin, expense vouchers (if you approve them).
4. **Alerts** from the automation agents — open, act, or assign.

## 2. Weekly routine (30 minutes)

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Monday | Review *Pending entries by person* with the team. Ask each person to clear their list. |
| Monday | Payables due this week — decide payments. |
| Wednesday | Quotation pipeline: Sent / Follow-up / Won / Lost this month; reasons for losses. |
| Friday | Project margins: open each active project, check spend vs contract value. |
| Friday | Stale prices: ask purchase to refresh pipe rates if many are flagged. |

## 3. Setup tasks (once, then occasionally)

1. **Users** — Admin → Users: create one login per person with the right role (sales, purchase, accounts, engineer, store). Never share logins — accountability depends on it.
2. **Company details** — Settings → Company: name, address, GSTIN, bank details, logo, quotation terms (needed on quotation/invoice prints).
3. **Pricing settings** — Settings → Pricing: default markup (proposed 40 %), minimum margin (proposed 15 %), quote validity (30 days), GST default (18 %). *These defaults are assumptions until you confirm them.*
4. **Agents** — Settings → Agents: turn agents on/off, change thresholds (e.g. stale price days for pipes), set reminder e-mail recipients.
5. **E-mail** — Office 365 mailbox details are configured by Sirah Digital (you need to send the mailbox details — action A-04).
6. **Price list** — check that the master price list import is complete; fill missing prices (fire alarm by make, extinguishers).

## 4. What the automation agents will tell you

| Agent | What you'll see |
|---|---|
| Margin Guard | Quotes with a line below minimum margin or below cost — approve or send back |
| Data Hygiene | Who has not logged follow-ups, won quotes without sales orders, delivered orders not invoiced, unapproved expenses |
| Project Budget | Projects at 80 % / 100 % of budget or negative margin |
| Pending Delivery | Overdue PO lines (critical after 7 days) |
| Receivables Dunning | Customers who received 4 reminders and still haven't paid — call them personally |
| Payables Due | Vendor invoices due within 5 days |
| All others | You see every alert; your team acts on their own |

Use **Agents → Run now** after a big data-entry session to refresh alerts immediately (they also run automatically every morning at 7:00).

## 5. What YOU must enter so the system works

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Approve/reject below-margin quotes | Same day | Sales cannot send them otherwise |
| Contract value and budget for each project | When the order is won | Without it, project margin can't be calculated |
| Pricing defaults and agent thresholds | Once | Drives every quotation |
| Weekly review of pending entries | Every week | The single biggest factor in whether the system succeeds |

## 6. Meeting Notes

Menu → *Meeting Notes*: record decisions from customer/vendor meetings and internal reviews, with action items linked to a customer or project.
