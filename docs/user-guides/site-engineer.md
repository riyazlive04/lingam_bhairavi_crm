# User Guide — Site Engineer

For: engineers doing installation, testing and commissioning at customer sites (system role **engineer**). Designed for your **mobile phone**. Open the CRM link in Chrome and choose "Add to Home screen" so it opens like an app.

Why your entries matter: the owner wants to know for each project **how much we received vs how much we spent** — material, purchases, and also **your petrol, lodging and other site expenses** (kickoff meeting [24:10–24:35]). Those expenses only exist in the system if you enter them.

## 1. Daily workflow

### Log an expense (under 1 minute)
1. Home → **+ Expense**.
2. Amount (₹), category: **Petrol**, **Lodging**, **Food**, **Travel**, **Local purchase**, **Other**.
3. **Project** — pick the site (your recent projects are on top).
4. Take a **photo of the bill** (camera opens directly).
5. Note (optional): e.g. "Hosur → Krishnagiri site, 2 days".
6. **Submit**. Accounts/owner approves it; then it is added to the project cost.

**One trip for two projects?** Turn on **Split** and divide the amount — e.g. petrol ₹2,000: Project X 50 % (₹1,000), Project Y 50 % (₹1,000). The split must add up to 100 %.

**Lodging for several days?** Enter one expense for the total bill with the dates in the note, or one per day — whichever matches the receipt.

### Confirm material received at site
Projects → your project → **Deliveries / Pending lines**: when material arrives at site, mark the quantity received (or tell store if something is missing). This stops items from being forgotten — the problem where 9 of 10 items arrive and the 10th is never followed up.

### Extinguisher refills / service at site
Refill Tracker → customer → select extinguishers serviced (e.g. 12 × ABC 6 kg, Safex) → **Mark refilled** with the date. The next due date moves forward automatically (1 year or 3 years).

### Project view
Your project page shows the site address, customer contact, materials delivered/pending, and your submitted expenses with their status.

## 2. What the automation agents will remind you about

| Agent | Reminder |
|---|---|
| Pending Delivery | Material for your project that is overdue or arriving today/tomorrow |
| Project Budget | Your project is at 80 % / 100 % of its budget (so you know to control spend) |
| Data Hygiene | Expenses you submitted that are still waiting for approval (so you can follow up), or project with no cost entries |
| Refill Due | Extinguishers due for refill (if you handle refills) |

## 3. What you MUST enter so the system works

| Entry | When | If you skip it… |
|---|---|---|
| **Every site expense** with the right project and a bill photo | Same day, on your phone | Project looks more profitable than it is; your reimbursement may be delayed |
| Split for shared trips | At entry | One project carries the whole cost |
| Material received at site (or missing items) | On arrival | Pending items are forgotten |
| Refill done + date | On completion | Next year's refill reminder is wrong |

Tip: enter the expense **before you leave the petrol pump or the lodge counter** — it takes less than a minute and you won't lose the bill.
